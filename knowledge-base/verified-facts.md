# SSF Verified Facts — Master Reference

## 1. Contribution rates (rate registry seed data)

### 1.1 Formal sector — total 31% of basic salary

Source: Scheme Operation Procedure 2075 §25 (5th Amendment, approved २०८१।०९।१०, effective २०८२।०१।०१)

| Component | Rate | Detail |
|-----------|------|--------|
| Employee (deducted) | 11% | Provident fund 10% + social security tax 1% |
| Employer (added) | 20% | Provident fund 10% + gratuity 8.33% + other 1.67% |
| **Total** | **31%** | of basic salary (आधारभूत पारिश्रमिक) |

**Scheme allocation (5th Amendment):**

| Scheme | Rate |
|--------|------|
| Medical treatment, health & maternity | 1.20% |
| Accident & disability | 0.80% |
| Dependent family | 0.67% |
| Old age (pension 20% + retirement benefit 8.33%) | 28.33% |

### 1.2 Informal sector — total 20.37% of minimum basic remuneration

Source: Informal Sector & Self-Employed Scheme Operation Procedure 2079 §5 (1st Amendment)

| Component | Rate |
|-----------|------|
| Worker | 11% |
| Government of Nepal | 9.37% |
| **Total** | **20.37%** |
| → Medical + accident + dependent | 10.37% |
| → Old age | 10% |

If the government (federal, province, or local) does not deposit its 9.37%, the contributor may pay the balance and participate.

### 1.3 Self-employed — 31% of a chosen base (1×–3× minimum basic remuneration)

Source: same procedure, §5(kha)

| Scheme | Rate |
|--------|------|
| Medical, health & maternity | 2.4% |
| Accident & disability | 0.80% |
| Dependent family | 1.80% |
| Old age (of which min. 16% to pension) | 26% |

### 1.4 Foreign employment — minimum 21.33% of industrial minimum basic remuneration (up to 3×)

Source: Foreign Employment Scheme Operation Procedure 2079 §6 (1st Amendment २०८०।११।१७)

| Allocation | Rate |
|------------|------|
| Medical/health/maternity + accident/disability + dependent family | 7.48% |
| Old age | 13.85% |

Registration happens when taking labour approval (श्रम स्वीकृति); those already abroad can apply through the online system.

## 3. Scheme benefit parameters (calculator/eligibility seed data)

### Medical, health & maternity (Procedure 2075, Chapter 2)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Qualifying period | 3 of the last 6 months (new contributors: after first 3 months; cover lasts 3 months after stopping) |
| Critical illness qualifying period | 24 of the last 28 months (cover lasts 24 months after stopping; 7 years for pensioners) |
| Inpatient ceiling | NPR 100,000/year (family total) |
| OPD ceiling | NPR 20,000/year (formal); NPR 25,000 (informal & foreign employment) |
| Co-payment | 20% of every claim |
| 60-month contributor top-up | 50% of NPR 1–10 lakh inpatient costs at networked hospitals paid by Fund |
| Family accident treatment | up to NPR 100,000 (spouse/child under 18) |
| Critical illness lifetime ceiling | NPR 1,000,000 (diagnosis ≤1 lakh; foreign treatment ≤7 lakh; re-examination ≤2 lakh) |
| Newborn care allowance | 1 month's minimum remuneration per child (incl. stillbirth/abortion after 24 weeks; one parent only) |
| Sick-leave payment | 60% of basic salary (less 2.67%) beyond 12 employer-paid days; max 13 weeks/year |
| Maternity-leave payment | 60% of basic salary beyond employer-paid 60 days; total ≤ 98 days |
| Post-retirement continuation | 1% of monthly pension for lifelong medical coverage (60 yrs + 180 months) |
| Exclusions | non-accident plastic/dental surgery, bariatric surgery, illegal abortion, epidemic suspension, double-claim with accident scheme |

### Accident & disability (Chapter 3)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Coverage start | First contribution (occupational disease: after 2 years) |
| Employment accident | Full treatment cost; report within 7 days; non-networked hospital without notice → max NPR 700,000 |
| Non-employment accident | Max NPR 700,000 (nil if other insurance pays ≥ 7 lakh) |
| Temporary total incapacity | 60% of basic salary monthly until return to work (>3 months needs committee endorsement) |
| Permanent disability pension | 60% of basic salary × disability %; lifelong; inflation-adjusted; ≤20% disability may be lump-sum |
| Permanent total disability | 60% of basic salary lifelong; floor = 60% of minimum basic remuneration; caregiver payment possible |
| Disability review | Every 5 years; final at age 58 |

### Dependent family (Chapter 4)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Qualifying period | Employment death: from day 1; other death: 12 months of contributions (5th Amendment) |
| Spouse pension | 60% of last basic salary, lifelong; stops on remarriage/alternative employment |
| Children's education allowance | 40% of last basic salary shared by up to 2 children <18 (21 if studying; no limit if disabled) |
| Dependent parents | 60% shared, lifelong (only if no spouse/child) |
| Funeral costs | NPR 25,000 |

### Old age (Chapter 5)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Structure | Pension scheme 20% + retirement benefit scheme 8.33% |
| Pension formula | (deposits + investment returns) ÷ **160** = lifelong monthly pension |
| Pension eligibility | Age 60 + 180 months of contributions |
| Mandatory pension membership | Contributions started on/after २०७८ साउन १ |
| <180 months at 60 | Choice: lump sum or ÷160 pension |
| Death before 60 | Heirs receive deposits + returns lump sum |
| Death within 7 years of pension | Spouse: same amount to 7-year mark, then 50% lifelong; else children <18 share 50% |
| High-earner split | Above 5× minimum remuneration → excess to retirement scheme (§23a) |
| Foreign nationals | Lump-sum withdrawal at end of employment (§24a) |
| Work past 60 | Contributions continue into retirement scheme (§24e) |
| Other pensions | No bar on receiving both (§24d) |

### Loans (Contributor Loan Directive 2079, 1st Amendment २०८१।०८।०२)

| Loan | Eligibility | Ceiling | Term |
|------|-------------|---------|------|
| Home | 36 months | NPR 7,500,000 (≤ collateral value, 15 yrs salary, or salary to age 60) | 20 years |
| Education | 36 months | NPR 3,500,000 or actual cost | 15 years |
| Social-work swap | existing loan swap | within collateral | per terms |
| Special | 36 months + ≥2 yrs to retirement | 80% of retirement-benefit balance (no collateral) | deduction/lump sum |

Combined loans (except special) capped at 15 years' salary. Scholarship: 24 months of contributions → actual fees or NPR 500,000 for children's bachelor-level study.

## 5. Canonical FAQ answers (chatbot seed, from study material Part 15.2)

1. **Who must list in SSF?** All employers (and their workers) in sectors designated by ministry gazette notice; new workers within 3 months of employment.
2. **Does 31% reduce the worker's salary?** Of the worker's 11%, 10% was already going to provident fund — the net new burden is 1%. The employer's 20% is added on top, not deducted. All of it returns as worker benefit and savings.
3. **Is money refunded after quitting a job?** Retirement-benefit scheme money (8.33% + voluntary + transfers) is paid out at job end/retirement. Pension-scheme money (20%) pays a monthly pension from 60; those with <180 months choose lump sum or pension. Foreign nationals may withdraw fully.
4. **How much pension?** (Pension deposits + returns) ÷ 160, monthly for life, inflation-adjusted per actuarial reports.
5. **Can I get treatment anywhere?** Networked hospitals are cashless/easiest. For employment accidents at non-networked hospitals without 7-day notification, the Fund pays at most NPR 7 lakh. Payment rates follow the 2076 hospital-payment procedure.
6. **What if the employer doesn't deposit?** Recovered with 10% interest; account/property freezes, license suspension, passport holds possible. During the default period the employer personally owes accident/death benefits. The worker can complain to the Fund.
7. **Family entitlement when a pensioner dies?** Within 7 years of pension start: spouse gets the same amount till the 7-year mark, then 50% for life; if no spouse, children under 18 share 50%.
8. **Why should migrant workers join?** ~21.33% of the industrial minimum wage secures family medical cover in Nepal, personal accident cover, and an old-age pension after returning. Listing happens with the labour permit.
9. **Does the government guarantee the Fund?** Yes — Act §62 obliges the Government of Nepal to continue the schemes if the Fund's resources are insufficient.
10. **Can one person receive both a government pension and an SSF pension?** Yes (Procedure §24d).
