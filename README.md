# SSF Guide — Social Security Fund, Nepal 🇳🇵

A practical, easy-to-follow guide to Nepal's **Social Security Fund (SSF / सामाजिक सुरक्षा कोष)** — covering registration, contribution rates, benefit schemes, and how to make claims.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** This guide is for general information only. Rates, rules, and procedures change over time. Always verify the latest details on the official SSF website ([ssf.gov.np](https://ssf.gov.np)) and the online system ([sosys.ssf.gov.np](https://sosys.ssf.gov.np)) before acting.

## 🚀 Quick Facts

- **Legal basis:** Contribution Based Social Security Act, 2074 (2017)
- **Total contribution:** **31%** of basic salary — 11% from the employee + 20% from the employer
- **Scheme allocation (5th Amendment, eff. २०८२):** Medical 1.20% · Accident 0.80% · Dependent 0.67% · Old Age 28.33%
- **Pension:** (deposits + returns) ÷ 160, monthly for life — age 60 + 180 months of contributions
- **Deposit deadline:** within 25 days of each Nepali month's end (10% interest on late payment)
- **Four schemes:** Medical & Maternity · Accident & Disability · Dependent Family · Old Age
- **Who it covers:** Formal-sector employees, and (in later phases) informal-sector workers, self-employed persons, and Nepali workers in foreign employment
- **Official portal:** [ssf.gov.np](https://ssf.gov.np) · Operating system: [sosys.ssf.gov.np](https://sosys.ssf.gov.np)
