# 5. Claims Process

All claims are filed through the SSF online system ([sosys.ssf.gov.np](https://sosys.ssf.gov.np)) — by the contributor, the employer, or (for death benefits) the family/nominee. Payments are made directly to the claimant's bank account.

## 5.1 General Steps

1. **Log in** to SOSYS with the contributor's SSN (or the employer's ID).
2. Choose **Claim** and select the relevant scheme/benefit.
3. **Fill the claim form** and upload supporting documents (see below).
4. Submit — SSF verifies the claim, may request corrections, then **approves and pays** to the registered bank account.
5. Track the claim status in SOSYS; SSF also notifies by SMS/email.

## 5.2 Documents by Claim Type

| Claim | Typical documents |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Medical treatment** | Hospital bills and receipts, prescription, discharge summary, diagnosis report |
| **Maternity** | Delivery/discharge report, birth certificate of the child, hospital bills |
| **Workplace accident** | Employer's accident report, medical reports and bills, police report where applicable |
| **Disability pension** | Disability assessment/percentage certificate from the authorized medical board |
| **Dependent family (death)** | Death certificate, relationship certificate (नाता प्रमाणित), nominee/claimant ID and bank details |
| **Old-age pension / lump sum** | Retirement/last-day-of-work evidence, citizenship, bank details |

## 5.3 Time Limits

- File claims **as soon as possible** after the event — the directives set submission windows for some benefits (e.g., medical claims are commonly expected within a set number of months of treatment).
- Keep **original bills and reports** until the claim is fully settled.

## 5.4 Employer Responsibilities in Claims

- Report **workplace accidents** to SSF promptly.
- Verify/forward employee claims where the process requires employer confirmation.
- Keep employee details (salary, status changes, exits) up to date in SOSYS — outdated records are the most common cause of claim delays.

## 5.5 If a Claim Is Rejected

1. Check the rejection reason in SOSYS — most rejections are for missing or unclear documents and can be corrected and resubmitted.
2. Contact SSF support (contact details at [ssf.gov.np](https://ssf.gov.np)) or visit an SSF office.
3. The Act provides for a formal **appeal/grievance** process if you disagree with a decision.
