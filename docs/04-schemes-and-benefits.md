# 4. Schemes & Benefits

SSF operates **four schemes**. Every contributor is covered by all four automatically — there is no separate opt-in.

> ⚠️ Benefit amounts, ceilings, and eligibility periods are set by the *Social Security Schemes Operational Directives* and are revised from time to time. The figures below describe the general design — always confirm current limits at [ssf.gov.np](https://ssf.gov.np).

## 4.1 Medical Treatment, Health & Maternity Protection Scheme

**Eligibility:** Generally available after contributions have been made for a minimum qualifying period (e.g., 3 months, and 12 months within the last 18 for maternity).

**Covers:**

- Hospital admission, treatment, and surgery costs (doctor fees, diagnostics, medicines, hospital charges) up to an annual ceiling.
- Outpatient treatment up to a set limit.
- **Maternity:** prenatal check-ups, delivery (including caesarean), and post-natal care for the contributor or the contributor's spouse, plus a lump-sum newborn-care allowance.
- **Sick leave income support:** a portion (e.g., 60%) of basic salary for hospitalized days beyond the employee's paid leave.

## 4.2 Accident & Disability Protection Scheme

**Eligibility:** From the first contribution (work-related accidents are covered immediately).

**Covers:**

- **Full medical costs** of treatment for workplace accidents and occupational diseases (no ceiling for employment-related accidents; non-work accidents up to a set limit).
- **Temporary disability:** income replacement (e.g., 60% of basic salary) while unable to work.
- **Permanent disability:** a lifelong monthly pension proportional to the degree of disability (e.g., 60% of basic salary × disability percentage).

## 4.3 Dependent Family Protection Scheme

**Covers, if a contributor dies:**

- **Spouse pension:** a lifelong monthly pension (e.g., 60% of the contributor's last basic salary) to the surviving spouse (until remarriage, if applicable).
- **Children's education allowance:** a monthly benefit (e.g., 40% of basic salary for one child, 60% shared for two or more) until age 18 (or 21 if studying).
- **Dependent parents' pension** where there is no spouse or child.
- **Funeral expenses:** a one-time lump sum to the family.

## 4.4 Old Age Protection Scheme

This is the largest scheme — **28.33% of basic salary** accumulates here each month, split conceptually into the pension fund (20%) and retirement/gratuity fund (8.33%).

**Benefits:**

- **Pension:** After reaching **age 60** with at least **180 months (15 years) of contributions**, the contributor receives a **lifelong monthly pension**, calculated by dividing the total accumulated amount (with returns) by a set divisor (e.g., 160).
- **Lump-sum retirement benefit:** Contributors who reach 60 without completing the minimum contribution period receive their accumulated balance (with returns) as a lump sum.
- **Portability & withdrawal rules:** Certain portions (e.g., the 8.33% gratuity element) may be withdrawable in specific situations (job loss, foreign employment departure) as per the prevailing directives; contributors may also be able to borrow against their balance under SSF's loan facilities (e.g., special loans, home loans) when offered.

## 4.5 Benefit Summary Table

| Event | Scheme | Benefit type |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| Illness / hospitalization | Medical & Maternity | Cost reimbursement + sick-pay |
| Childbirth | Medical & Maternity | Delivery costs + newborn allowance |
| Workplace accident | Accident & Disability | Full treatment + disability pension |
| Permanent disability | Accident & Disability | Lifelong pension |
| Death of contributor | Dependent Family | Spouse/children pension + funeral grant |
| Retirement at 60 (≥15 yrs contribution) | Old Age | Lifelong monthly pension |
| Retirement at 60 (<15 yrs contribution) | Old Age | Lump-sum refund with returns |
