# 4. Schemes & Benefits

SSF operates **four schemes** for formal-sector contributors. Every contributor is covered by all four automatically — there is no separate opt-in.

> 📜 **Source:** Social Security Scheme Operation Procedure, 2075 (with 5th Amendment, effective १ वैशाख २०८२). Figures below reflect that amendment; always confirm current limits at [ssf.gov.np](https://ssf.gov.np).

## 4.1 Medical Treatment, Health & Maternity Protection Scheme

**Eligibility (§4):** at least **3 months of contributions within the last 6 months**. New contributors qualify after their first 3 regular months; cover continues for 3 months after contributions stop. For **critical illness** treatment: at least **24 months of contributions within the last 28 months** (cover continues 24 months after stopping; 7 years for pensioners).

**Who is covered:** the contributor, their spouse, and children under 18 (maternity: the contributor or the contributor's wife).

**Benefits (§5–7):**

- **Inpatient treatment** (admission, surgery, diagnostics, medicines): up to **NPR 100,000 per year** for the whole family.
- **Outpatient (OPD/prescription)** treatment: up to **NPR 20,000 per year** (within the overall 1-lakh family ceiling).
- **Co-payment:** the contributor bears **20%** of every claim.
- **60-month contributors (5th Amendment):** for inpatient treatment at SSF-networked hospitals, the Fund pays **50% of costs between NPR 1 lakh and 10 lakh** directly to the hospital.
- **Family accident treatment (5th Amendment):** up to NPR 100,000 for accident treatment of a spouse or child under 18.
- **Critical illness** (heart bypass/open-heart surgery, stroke, kidney disease, cancer, Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, spinal/head injury, sickle-cell anaemia, haemophilia, thalassaemia, and other government-designated diseases): up to **NPR 1,000,000 over the whole service period**, with sub-limits (diagnosis costs up to 1 lakh; medical-board-referred foreign treatment up to 7 lakh; post-discharge re-examination up to 2 lakh).
- **Maternity:** pregnancy check-ups, delivery (incl. surgery), treatment up to 6 weeks post-delivery and infant care up to 3 months (within the limits above), plus a **newborn-care allowance of one month's minimum remuneration per child** (also payable for stillbirth/abortion after 24 weeks; only one parent may claim if both are contributors).
- **Sick-leave income:** 60% of basic salary (less the 2.67% scheme contribution) for hospitalized/prescribed home-treatment days beyond the employer's 12 paid sick-leave days — up to 13 weeks per year.
- **Maternity-leave income:** 60% of basic salary for the maternity-leave period beyond the employer-paid 60 days, up to 98 days in total.

**Not covered (§8):** non-accident plastic surgery and dental treatment, bariatric surgery, abortion costs (except as legally allowed), suspension of the scheme during a nationwide epidemic, or costs already covered by the accident scheme.

**After retirement (§8a):** pensioners with 180+ months of contributions who are 60+ can pay **1% of their monthly pension** to keep lifelong medical coverage.

## 4.2 Accident & Disability Protection Scheme

**Eligibility:** covered **from the first contribution**. Occupational-disease treatment requires **2 years of contributions** (cover continues 2 years after stopping).

**Benefits:**

- **Employment-related accident or occupational disease:** the Fund bears the **entire treatment cost**. The accident must be reported to SSF within **7 days** (message/email accepted). Treatment at a non-networked hospital without notification is covered only up to NPR 700,000.
- **Non-employment accident:** treatment costs up to **NPR 700,000** (not payable if another insurance already covers ≥ 7 lakh).
- **Temporary total incapacity:** 60% of basic salary monthly (less 2.67% scheme contribution) until return to work; beyond 3 months requires Health Examination Committee endorsement.
- **Permanent disability:** lifelong monthly pension = **60% of basic salary × disability percentage** (inflation-adjusted). Disability of 20% or less can be paid as a lump sum. The percentage is set by the Health Examination Committee and reviewed every 5 years (final review at age 58).
- **Permanent total disability:** 60% of basic salary monthly for life — never less than 60% of the government minimum basic remuneration; payable to a caregiver on local-government recommendation if the contributor cannot move about.

## 4.3 Dependent Family Protection Scheme

**Eligibility:** death from an **employment-related accident** is covered from the first contribution; death from **other causes** requires at least **12 months of regular contributions** (5th Amendment).

**Benefits:**

| Benefit | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Spouse pension | **60% of the contributor's last basic salary**, monthly for life (inflation-adjusted); stops on remarriage or alternative employment |
| Children's education allowance | **40% of the last basic salary**, shared among up to **2 children under 18** (until 21 if continuously studying; no age limit for disabled children) |
| Dependent parents | 60% of basic salary shared, for life — only where there is no spouse or child |
| Funeral expenses | **NPR 25,000** lump sum to the nearest family member/heir |

## 4.4 Old Age Protection Scheme

**28.33% of basic salary** accumulates here monthly, in two parts:

| Part | Rate | Benefit |
|------|------|---------|
| **Pension scheme** | 20% | Lifelong monthly pension |
| **Retirement benefit scheme** | 8.33% | Lump sum at retirement / end of employment |

**Pension (§20–22):**

- **Formula:** (total deposits + investment returns) **÷ 160** = monthly pension, for life.
- **Eligibility:** age **60** + at least **180 months** of contributions.
- Everyone who started contributing on or after **१ साउन २०७८** is automatically in the pension scheme.
- **At 60 with fewer than 180 months:** choose either a **lump sum** (with returns) or the ÷160 lifelong monthly pension.
- **Death before pension age:** the nominee/heir receives all deposits plus returns as a lump sum.
- **Death after pension starts (§24c):** if within 7 years of the pension starting, the spouse receives the same amount until the 7-year mark, then 50% for life; without a spouse, dependent children under 18 share 50%.

**Retirement benefit (§23):** the 8.33% portion, voluntary extra contributions, and balances transferred from old PF/gratuity funds are paid as a **lump sum** at retirement or end of employment (to the heir on death). Contributors with 3 continuous years of contributions can take a **special loan of up to 80%** of this balance.

**Other rules:**

- **High earners (§23a):** on salary above 5× the minimum remuneration, only the minimum-remuneration portion goes to the pension scheme; the excess goes to the retirement benefit scheme. Retirement-scheme funds can be moved into the pension scheme to raise the pension.
- **Foreign nationals (§24a):** may withdraw the old-age balance as a lump sum when employment ends (same for those renouncing Nepali citizenship).
- **Working past 60 (§24e):** contributions can continue; new post-60 contributions accumulate in the retirement benefit scheme.
- **Other pensions (§24d):** receiving a government or other pension does not bar an SSF pension.

## 4.5 Contributor Loan Facilities (Loan Directive, 2079)

| Loan | Eligibility | Ceiling | Term |
|------|-------------|---------|------|
| Home loan | 36 months of contributions | NPR 7.5 million (within collateral value, 15 years' salary, or salary remaining to age 60 — whichever is lower) | up to 20 years |
| Education loan | 36 months of contributions | NPR 3.5 million or actual institutional cost, whichever is lower | up to 15 years |
| Social-work loan swap | swap of an existing loan from another fund | within collateral value | per swap terms |
| Special loan | 36 months of contributions + ≥ 2 years to retirement | 80% of the retirement-benefit balance (no collateral needed) | salary deduction or lump sum |

Contributors with 24+ months of contributions can also apply for the **children's higher-education scholarship** (actual fees or NPR 500,000, whichever is lower) funded from the national welfare fund.

## 4.6 Benefit Summary Table

| Event | Scheme | Benefit |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Illness / hospitalization | Medical & Maternity | Up to 1 lakh/yr inpatient + 20k OPD (20% co-pay) + sick pay |
| Critical illness | Medical & Maternity | Up to 10 lakh over service period |
| Childbirth | Medical & Maternity | Delivery costs + 1 month minimum wage per child + maternity-leave pay |
| Workplace accident | Accident & Disability | Full treatment cost + disability pension |
| Non-work accident | Accident & Disability | Treatment up to 7 lakh |
| Permanent disability | Accident & Disability | 60% × disability % monthly, for life |
| Death of contributor | Dependent Family | Spouse 60% + children 40% + NPR 25,000 funeral |
| Retirement at 60 (≥180 months) | Old Age | (Deposits + returns) ÷ 160 monthly for life + 8.33% lump sum |
| Retirement at 60 (<180 months) | Old Age | Lump sum or ÷160 pension, contributor's choice |
