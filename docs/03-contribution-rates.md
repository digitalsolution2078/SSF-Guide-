# 3. Contribution Rates

## 3.1 Who Pays What

The total monthly contribution is **31% of the employee's basic salary**:

| Payer | Rate | Made up of |
|-------|------|------------|
| **Employee** | **11%** | 10% provident fund + 1% social security tax |
| **Employer** | **20%** | 10% provident fund + 8.33% gratuity + 1.67% other |
| **Total** | **31%** | |

> 💡 The contribution is calculated on **basic salary** only — not on allowances, overtime, or bonuses. The basic salary used cannot be lower than the government-set minimum basic remuneration.

## 3.2 How the 31% Is Allocated Across Schemes

Allocation per the **Social Security Scheme Operation Procedure 2075, 5th Amendment** (approved २०८१।०९।१०, effective from १ वैशाख २०८२):

| Scheme | Allocation (of basic salary) |
|--------|------------------------------|
| Medical Treatment, Health & Maternity Protection | 1.20% |
| Accident & Disability Protection | 0.80% |
| Dependent Family Protection | 0.67% |
| Old Age Protection (pension 20% + retirement benefit 8.33%) | 28.33% |
| **Total** | **31%** |

The bulk of the contribution (28.33%) accumulates in the contributor's **old-age account** — split into the pension scheme (20%) and the retirement benefit scheme (8.33%) — while the small remainder funds the three insurance-type protections.

## 3.3 Worked Example

For an employee with a **basic salary of NPR 30,000/month**:

| Item | Calculation | Amount (NPR) |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Employee contribution (11%) | 30,000 × 0.11 | 3,300 |
| Employer contribution (20%) | 30,000 × 0.20 | 6,000 |
| **Total deposited to SSF** | 30,000 × 0.31 | **9,300** |
| → Old age protection | 30,000 × 0.2833 | 8,499 |
| → Medical & maternity | 30,000 × 0.012 | 360 |
| → Accident & disability | 30,000 × 0.008 | 240 |
| → Dependent family | 30,000 × 0.0067 | 201 |

The employee's **take-home pay is reduced by NPR 3,300**; the employer bears NPR 6,000 on top of the salary.

## 3.4 Deadlines & Penalties

- Contributions must be deposited within **25 days after the end of each Nepali month** (Act §4, as amended by the Some Nepal Acts Amendment Act, 2082).
- Late payment attracts an additional charge of **10% interest** on the outstanding amount.
- Against non-listing or non-depositing employers, the Executive Director can initiate action up to **freezing bank accounts and property, suspending licenses and concessions, and withholding passports** (Act §9). If a worker suffers an accident or dies during a period of unpaid contributions, the **employer itself must pay** the equivalent benefit.

## 3.5 Rates for Other Contributor Groups

| Group | Contribution | Allocation |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| **Informal sector worker** | 20.37% of minimum basic remuneration (worker 11% + Government of Nepal 9.37%) | 10.37% to medical + accident + dependent; 10% to old age |
| **Self-employed** | 31% of a chosen base between 1× and 3× the minimum basic remuneration | Medical 2.4% · Accident 0.80% · Dependent 1.80% · Old age 26% (min. 16% to pension) |
| **Foreign employment** | Minimum 21.33% of the industrial minimum basic remuneration (up to 3×) | 7.48% to medical + accident + dependent; 13.85% to old age |

*(Source: Informal Sector & Self-Employed Scheme Operation Procedure 2079 §5; Foreign Employment Scheme Operation Procedure 2079 §6, both with 1st amendments.)*

## 3.6 Tax Treatment

- Contributions to SSF are **deductible from taxable income** within the limits set by the Income Tax Act (currently up to one-third of assessable income or the prescribed cap, whichever is lower — confirm the current cap with your tax advisor).
- Employees contributing to SSF are exempt from the 1% social security tax on the first income slab.
