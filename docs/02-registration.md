# 2. Registration (Listing) in SSF

Registration in SSF is called **listing (सूचीकरण)**. It happens in two steps: first the **employer** is listed, then the employer lists each **employee**.

## 2.1 Employer Registration

**Where:** [sosys.ssf.gov.np](https://sosys.ssf.gov.np) → *Employer Registration*

**Typical documents required:**

- Company / firm registration certificate
- PAN / VAT registration certificate
- Contact details of the organization and its authorized person
- Number of employees

**Steps:**

1. Open the SOSYS portal and choose employer registration.
2. Fill in the organization's details and upload the required documents.
3. Submit the application. SSF verifies the details and issues an **Employer ID** with login credentials.
4. Log in and proceed to list your employees.

## 2.2 Employee Registration

**Who does it:** The employer, through its SOSYS login. (Employees in the informal sector, self-employed persons, and foreign-employment workers can register themselves.)

**Typical documents / details required per employee:**

- Citizenship certificate (or passport / national ID)
- Passport-size photo
- Appointment or contract details (position, joining date, basic salary)
- Contact details and family/nominee details

**Steps:**

1. Employer logs in to SOSYS and selects *Contributor Registration*.
2. Enter the employee's personal, family, and employment details; upload documents.
3. On approval, the employee receives a **Social Security Number (SSN)** — a unique lifetime ID.
4. The employee can then log in to SOSYS with their SSN to view contributions and update details.

## 2.3 After Registration — Monthly Filing

Every month the employer must:

1. **Declare the payroll** (contribution detail) for all listed employees in SOSYS.
2. **Deposit the total contribution (31% of basic salary)** within **15 days of the end of each Nepali month**.
3. Late deposits attract **interest/penalty of 10% per annum** on the due amount.

## 2.4 Points to Remember

- Listing is **mandatory** for formal-sector employers — operating without listing can lead to fines under the Act.
- An employee keeps the **same SSN for life**, even when changing jobs; the new employer simply adds the existing SSN to its payroll.
- Employees can check their contribution history anytime by logging in to SOSYS or through the SSF mobile app.
