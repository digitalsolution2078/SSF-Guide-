# 1. Introduction to the Social Security Fund (SSF)

## What is SSF?

The **Social Security Fund (सामाजिक सुरक्षा कोष)** is Nepal's contribution-based social security system. Employers and employees both contribute a percentage of the employee's basic salary every month, and in return the employee (the *contributor*) receives protection through four social security schemes — from medical treatment and maternity care to a pension in old age.

The scheme was formally launched on **27 November 2018 (मंसिर ११, २०७५)**, with mandatory enrollment for formal-sector employers rolled out in phases afterwards.

## Legal Basis

| Instrument | Year | Purpose |
|------------|------|---------|
| Contribution Based Social Security Act, 2074 | 2017 | Establishes the Fund and makes contributions mandatory |
| Social Security Regulation, 2075 | 2018 | Operational rules for the Fund |
| Social Security Schemes Operational Directives | 2075 (updated periodically) | Detailed benefit rules for each scheme |
| Labour Act, 2074 | 2017 | Requires provident fund, gratuity, and insurance — now channelled through SSF |

## Who Must Join?

- **Employers** in the formal private sector must list (register) themselves and all of their employees in the Fund.
- **Employees** with any employment relationship — permanent, contract, part-time, or piece-rate — must be listed by their employer.
- **Voluntary coverage** has been progressively extended to:
  - Workers in the **informal sector**
  - **Self-employed** persons
  - Nepali citizens in **foreign employment** (migrant workers)

## Why SSF Instead of Separate PF / Gratuity / Insurance?

Before SSF, the Labour Act required employers to manage provident fund, gratuity, medical insurance, and accident insurance separately. SSF consolidates all of these obligations into a single monthly contribution, giving employees:

1. **Portability** — the SSF account follows the worker between jobs.
2. **A lifelong pension** rather than only a lump sum at retirement.
3. **Insurance-type protections** (medical, maternity, accident, disability, dependent family) funded from a small share of the contribution.

## Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Contributor (योगदानकर्ता)** | An employee (or self-employed person) enrolled in SSF |
| **Listing (सूचीकरण)** | Registration of an employer or employee in the Fund |
| **Basic salary** | The base remuneration used to calculate contributions (excludes allowances) |
| **SSN / SSF ID** | The unique Social Security Number issued to each contributor |
| **SOSYS** | The Fund's online operating system: [sosys.ssf.gov.np](https://sosys.ssf.gov.np) |
